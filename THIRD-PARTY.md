## Bambuddy icon

`app.ico` is a multi-resolution conversion of `static/img/favicon.png` from
[maziggy/bambuddy](https://github.com/maziggy/bambuddy), retrieved from commit
`ea3f2645` during development. The image design was not changed.
The upstream project distributes its source under GNU AGPL version 3; a copy is in `LICENSE`.
The Bambuddy name and logo identify the server this unofficial client connects to.
This client is not an official Bambuddy project or an endorsement by its maintainers.
